## Architectural quality contribution

The quality contribution maintains system-wide requirements while reporting implementation evidence for a smaller subset. M2 requires gate processing within two seconds per read under the stated peak workload, no loss of movement or health records in normal operation, and RBAC with comprehensive audit logging. Connectivity loss during gate processing and inconvenient, error-prone vaccination entry motivate continuity and on-site mobile recording. The assignment still requires the group to implement at least one main module; the supporting authorization library and checker do not establish completion of that module. (M2, PDF pp.2–3; [E-SOURCE](02_Evidence_and_Contribution_Status.md#e-source), [E-RUNTIME](02_Evidence_and_Contribution_Status.md#e-runtime))

The R2 quality design traces NFR-01–08, SC-01–08, proposed user-error controls and QAS01–10 to the submitted M1 and M2 delta. It retains the proposed driver grouping of reliability/durability with explicit continuity, performance, and security/auditability; user error prevention is a proposed additional characteristic. It supplies criteria to the architecture owners without re-scoring alternatives or selecting a new style. The existing local-edge placement remains part of the M1 design. M1's retained measurement boundary is each required read's correct durable local outcome, with clock uncertainty accounted for; a percentile, suite elapsed time or matching total cannot establish the two-second requirement. The 60-minute outage and 600-second recovery envelopes are project-defined design budgets, not assignment-mandated SLAs. (M1, PDF pp.12–14, 20–24, 35–48; [E-DESIGN](02_Evidence_and_Contribution_Status.md#e-design))

Full six-field QAS09 and QAS10 remain in Quality Design §§6–7. QAS09 preserves option A, online-only clinical capture, and proposes option B, bounded local drafts with online final acceptance. Its 20-intent cohort, 120-second recovery measure and maximum 30-minute capture eligibility are proposals awaiting owner review; the dashboard lease is a separate boundary. Offline A/B selection and relevant R0/H0/ADR acceptance are not evidenced as closed. QAS10 distinguishes human authority from cattle identity, requires current authorization and custody ordering, and preserves authorized history access for known deactivated cattle. Unknown tags remain unresolved; multiple tags and cattle/form mismatches require explicit handling. These design obligations remain even where runtime verification is unavailable. ([E-DESIGN](02_Evidence_and_Contribution_Status.md#e-design), [E-DECISIONS](02_Evidence_and_Contribution_Status.md#e-decisions))

## Implemented authorization boundary

The implementation is the Java 21 JAR `vn.edu.hcmut.vinabeef:access-policy:0.1.0-SNAPSHOT`. `ClinicalAuthorizationPolicy.evaluate(Action, Subject, ResourceContext, Instant)` returns `Decision(allowed, reason, matchedGrant)`. Nested immutable records defensively copy grant collections; the caller supplies server decision time. The module has no production dependencies, clock reads, JWT parsing, HTTP, SQL or audit persistence. Its actual code, usage example and dependency evidence are indexed under E-POLICY. ([E-POLICY](02_Evidence_and_Contribution_Status.md#e-policy), [E-POLICY-QA](02_Evidence_and_Contribution_Status.md#e-policy-qa))

Whole-grant additive RBAC requires one ACTIVE grant to match the entire role/action/farm/barn rule at server decision time in `[validFrom, validUntil)`, with an inclusive start and exclusive end: a matching ACTIVE grant is effective exactly at `validFrom` and is no longer effective at `validUntil`. If `validUntil` is absent, there is no fixed expiry, but authoritative current ACTIVE grants remain a caller precondition for every protected operation. Role and scope cannot be combined across grants. BS requires its current farm and assigned barn; an empty barn set is not a wildcard. FM uses current farm scope. SA/EM-only subjects receive no ordinary clinical privilege, while an independent valid BS/FM grant may allow. Revoked or expired grants neither contribute scope nor veto another valid grant. Allow decisions identify a matched grant reference/version; the lexicographically smallest matching reference is selected deterministically. ([E-DECISIONS](02_Evidence_and_Contribution_Status.md#e-decisions), [E-POLICY](02_Evidence_and_Contribution_Status.md#e-policy))

The four actions distinguish individual cattle lookup, new Health submission, per-record history and receipt reads. `HistoryRecord` and `AcceptedReceipt` require a record linked to the target cattle with a non-null origin farm. FM can read an own-origin record or its accepted receipt after transfer without obtaining a new-write or unrestricted destination-profile entitlement. `TargetReceipt`, for pending/failed outcomes without an accepted record, uses current target scope only. BS has no own-origin extension. Lifecycle is intentionally absent from permission inputs, preserving ordinary authorized identity/history scope for known deactivated cattle without claiming that tag resolution or returned history was integration-tested. (M1, PDF pp.9, 15–17; [E-POLICY](02_Evidence_and_Contribution_Status.md#e-policy))

Construction validates shape and identifier agreement, not authentication, grant freshness, actual DB associations or canonical custody. Verified identity and authoritative current grants at every protected operation are caller preconditions; unavailable authority must fail closed at the boundary. Health owns per-record filtering, immutable first-acceptance origin, version/dose/lifecycle checks, idempotency, source evidence and validation/commit ordering. Current permission may ALLOW while Health rejects changed payload or stale expected version. An already committed intent is resolved through current READ_RECEIPT authorization rather than reapplying the new-acceptance version guard. No delegated submit/adopt/reassign workflow is supplied. ([E-DECISIONS](02_Evidence_and_Contribution_Status.md#e-decisions), [E-POLICY](02_Evidence_and_Contribution_Status.md#e-policy), [E-RUNTIME](02_Evidence_and_Contribution_Status.md#e-runtime))

## Design principles and trade-offs

The evaluator has one responsibility: permission over semantic values. Identity, persistence and application workflows remain outside its dependency boundary, providing concrete SRP/DIP benefits. Separate resource variants avoid a large nullable request model and make action/context incompatibility explicit. Defensive copies and constructor invariants preserve the value contracts shared by those variants. The sealed model and enum switches favor deliberate review when new actions are added; this implementation does not claim unrestricted extension without modification. A shared JAR also introduces API/version coordination between producer and consumers. No provider interface, plugin registry or service was added solely to demonstrate a principle. ([E-POLICY](02_Evidence_and_Contribution_Status.md#e-policy))

## Limitations, reflection and AI declaration

Clinical T06/T07/T08/T09 remain **Not Run**, blocked by missing Health interfaces, controlled setup, complete exports and owner hooks. T05 remains Design-only/scope pending. Gate code and owner tests exist, but this contribution does not claim gate deadline/load/hardware, collector recovery or two-adapter acceptance. Main-module completion, owner adoption and final report approval are not established. ([E-RUNTIME](02_Evidence_and_Contribution_Status.md#e-runtime), [E-PLAN](02_Evidence_and_Contribution_Status.md#e-plan))

The work illustrates why permission and clinical acceptance need separate oracles: conflating them would make payload/version failures look like authorization decisions. Checker QA provided a second concrete lesson. The original 34 controls passed while singleton-array coercion caused false passes and a JSON `Keys` member caused false rejection. Explicit scalar checks and real dictionary-entry enumeration, followed by focused regressions and independent probes, corrected those tool defects; they were not clinical production bugs. The reviewer closed both findings in R2. This neutral reflection is a draft for Phúc to verify and adapt before adoption. ([E-R1-DEFECTS](02_Evidence_and_Contribution_Status.md#e-r1-defects), [E-QA-R2](02_Evidence_and_Contribution_Status.md#e-qa-r2))
